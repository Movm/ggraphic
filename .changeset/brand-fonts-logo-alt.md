A brand can set `serif` and `condensed` display fonts (falling back to the quote and headline fonts) and a second logo, `logoAlt`, chosen by how dark the ground is. `logoSizeOf` sizes whichever logo is swapped in.

A brand can set a `layout` (margin and logo corner and envelope); `brandLayout`, `safeArea` and `logoEnvelope` read it, and the defaults match today's seed geometry.
