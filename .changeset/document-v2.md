Canvas document version 2 for brand bindings. The core step 1 → 2 is the identity on pages; the page keys `bind`, `brandScheme` and `brandStamp` persist, are inherited by added and converted pages, and are known to the PPTX export. A build without version 2 now opens such a document read-only.
