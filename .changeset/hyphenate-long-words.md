Break a word that is wider than its text box at syllable boundaries with a hyphen instead of in the middle of a syllable, and add `fitFontSizeToSlot` to shrink a headline until it fits the room above the next text. The editor draws such text with the shared layout, so the canvas and the export wrap alike.
